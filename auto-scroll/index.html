Auto Scroll - Hands Free

Auto scroll that waits when you touch the screen and resumes when you let go.

Get it on Google Play

Most auto scrollers quit the moment you touch the screen. Tap a comment, nudge the brightness, and you have to start over.

Auto Scroll works the other way. Touch the screen and it steps aside. Let go and it picks up where it stopped.

It does not quit when you touch

Your touch pauses scrolling for a moment. Lift your finger and it resumes on its own after 0.5, 1.2 or 3 seconds, or only when you tap the button, if you prefer.

The button does not disappear

The floating button stays above the gesture bar, keeps working in full-screen apps, and comes back whenever you open the app. Long-press it to turn everything off.

Smooth, not page jumps

Instead of jumping a whole page, it chains short swipes, so reading long screens feels continuous.

Five settings, nothing else

Speed, resume delay, button size, button opacity, direction.

Pro (one payment, not a subscription)

Keep the screen on while scrolling, an auto-stop timer, and hiding the button while scrolling.

There are no ads in the free version or in Pro. No account, no analytics.

About the accessibility service

This app uses the Android AccessibilityService API to send scroll gestures to the app you are viewing, and to pause when you scroll yourself or a keyboard opens. The network permission in the list comes from Google Play's billing library for the Pro purchase. You can turn the service off by long-pressing the floating button or in Android settings.
