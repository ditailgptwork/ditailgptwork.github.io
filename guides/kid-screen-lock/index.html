How to stop a toddler from tapping the screen while a video plays (Android)

A video for a toddler often ends the same way: a small hand pauses it, opens another video, taps an ad or leaves the app. Here are three ways to stop that on an Android phone or tablet.

1. App pinning (built into Android)

Look for App pinning in Settings, usually under Security; the exact menu name changes from phone to phone. Pinning keeps the phone inside one app, so a child cannot leave it. Taps still work inside the app, so the video can still be paused or changed.

2. Interaction control (some Samsung Galaxy phones)

Some Galaxy phones include Interaction control under Settings › Accessibility › Interaction and dexterity. It blocks touches on the whole screen or on parts of it and is switched on with a shortcut you set up once.

3. Touch Lock (one tap, any Android phone)

  1. Open Touch Lock and tap Start. A small lock button floats over your apps.
  2. Play the video and tap the button. Every touch on the screen is now ignored, and the video keeps playing.
  3. Double-tap the screen to unlock.

Touch Lock Pro (one payment) adds a PIN unlock and a protection mode for children who learn the double-tap. The app uses only the “Display over other apps” permission and collects no personal data.

Which one to use

  • To keep a child inside one app: app pinning.
  • To block taps on a Galaxy phone without installing anything: Interaction control.
  • To block taps on any Android phone with one tap, and unlock just as fast: Touch Lock.
