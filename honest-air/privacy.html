Honest Air — Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · Developer: Ditail Labs · Contact: ditaillabs@gmail.com

Short version

Honest Air has no account, no analytics and no advertising SDK in this version. Your coordinates never leave your phone. We never sell or share location.

Location

If you choose “My location”, the app reads your device’s approximate (coarse) location and, on the device, picks the nearest city from a list built into the app. It then downloads the data file for that city’s country (for example, one file for all of Korea) and the world earthquake list, which is the same for everyone. The requests name only the country; they contain no coordinates. If you pick a city instead, no device location is read at all.

Where the data comes from

Our own pipeline collects public data every hour and publishes small static files: ground-station measurements from OpenAQ, Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) forecasts, weather from MET Norway, the Korea Meteorological Administration and the Japan Meteorological Agency, and earthquakes from the U.S. Geological Survey. Your phone talks only to the file host, never to these sources.

File hosts

The files are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and may later be served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any website, a host can see the network address of a request and which file was requested. We do not keep request logs, and the hosts’ own privacy policies apply to their logs.

Stored on your device only

The city you picked, the cities you saved, the last data downloaded, the theme and your alert setting are kept in the app’s private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

Alerts are optional and are created on your device when the grade changes. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
