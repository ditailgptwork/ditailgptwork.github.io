Honest Air — Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Developer: Ditail Labs · Contact: ditaillabs@gmail.com

Short version

Honest Air has no account, no analytics and no advertising SDK in this version. We do not run a server that stores your data, and we never sell or share your location for advertising.

Location

If you choose “Use my approximate location”, the app reads your device’s approximate (coarse) location. On the device, it rounds the coordinates to a 0.25° grid (about 25 km) and sends only those rounded coordinates to the air-quality data provider to get the data for that area. Nothing about your location is stored on any server we control. If you pick a city instead, no device location is read at all.

Data provider

During testing, air-quality data comes from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API, which uses Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) information. Like any website, the provider can see the network address of the request. Their own privacy policy applies to that request.

Stored on your device only

The city you picked, the last data downloaded and your alert setting are kept in the app’s private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

Alerts are optional and are created on your device when the grade changes. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
